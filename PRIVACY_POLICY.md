# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** June 14, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use this Fusion 360 Extension (the "Extension"). As an independent developer, I value your privacy and believe in minimizing data collection. Because this extension runs locally on your machine within Autodesk Fusion 360, your data stays where it belongs: with you.

---

## 1. Information Gathered by the Extension

### Local Processing Only
The Extension operates entirely locally on your workstation. It interacts directly with the Autodesk Fusion 360 API to perform its intended utility. 

* **Design and CAD Data:** The Extension reads or modifies geometry, parameters, paths, or active document data strictly to execute its function. **None** of this data is compiled, cached externally, or transmitted off your machine.
* **Personal Information:** The Extension does not ask for, collect, or store personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).

### No Cloud Transmission or Analytics
* The Extension does not utilize external tracking scripts, telemetry, cookies, or remote analytics frameworks.
* There is no remote backend server connected to this Extension; therefore, traffic logs and system metrics are never sent to the developer.

---

## 2. Autodesk Platform and Licensing Data

If this Extension is downloaded or purchased through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace (formerly the Autodesk App Store), Autodesk may handle transaction details, licensing verification, and account information under their own privacy standards. 

* **Entitlement API (If Applicable):** If licensing validation is enforced, the Extension queries the standard Autodesk Entitlement API using an encrypted token provided by Fusion 360 to verify a valid purchase or trial status. This process is fully managed by Autodesk infrastructure.
* Please review the [Autodesk Privacy Statement](https://www.autodesk.com/company/legal-notices-trademarks/privacy-statement) to understand how they govern your store data.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with, call upon, or share data with any third-party web services, advertising networks, or data brokers.

---

## 4. Data Security
Because no data is transferred or retained by the developer, there is no remote database or server file at risk of exposure. Your data is protected by the native local storage and security policies governing your individual computer and your Autodesk Fusion 360 environment.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This policy may be revised periodically to accurately reflect technical changes or updates to the Extension. Any updates will be pushed directly to this repository with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the file.

---

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding the technical behavior of this Extension or this Privacy Policy, please open an **Issue** directly in this GitHub repository.
